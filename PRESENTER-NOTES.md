# Creative Computing · IADT Open Day 2026

## Presenter notes (revised v2)

### 01. Creative Computing
Welcome students and families. Introduce DL836, a four-year, Level 8 honours degree. Creative Computing combines strong programming and software-development skills with design, creativity and an understanding of people. No portfolio is required to apply. The course portfolio is built during the programme.

### 02. What is Creative Computing?
Define Creative Computing in plain English. Students learn to program, design and build working products, with an emphasis on usability and iteration. There is serious computing here, not simply using creative software. You do not need a portfolio to apply.

### 03. Why IADT?
Explain the day-to-day learning approach: a mix of lectures, tutorials and labs, accessible lecturers, small-group support and project work. Students develop programming ability alongside communication, teamwork, independent problem-solving and creativity. Introduce the current student helper on the following slide.

### 04. Student perspective
Pause and invite the student helper to respond naturally. Suggested main question: “What’s it really like to study Creative Computing at IADT?” If a prompt is needed: What surprised you when you started? What has been your favourite project or module? How does the course compare with what you expected? What advice would you give someone considering applying? Allow roughly 2 minutes, or invite one question from the audience.

### 05. Your four-year journey
Four phases of learning: Year 1 builds foundations; Year 2 develops core technical and creative skills; Year 3 offers electives and professional practice; Year 4 focuses on advanced choices and an independent major project. These are developmental phases, not formal named pathways.

### 06. Year 1 Semester 1
YEAR 1 SEMESTER 1. Programming: Learn the building blocks of code and use them to solve problems. Computing Concepts: Understand how computers represent, process and communicate information. Digital Media Prototyping + Web Design: Turn initial ideas into web designs and digital media prototypes. IADT Connect: Collaborate across disciplines while developing creative thinking and college-study skills.

### 07. Year 1 Semester 2
YEAR 1 SEMESTER 2. Integrated Project: Bring your new skills together to plan and create a complete digital project. Introduction to Databases: Explore how information is organised, stored and retrieved. Web Development: Develop functional websites using the foundations of modern web technology. IADT Year 1 Workshop: Take on a practical workshop challenge through collaboration and experimentation.

### 08. Year 2 Semester 1
YEAR 2 SEMESTER 1. User Experience + Interaction Design: Explore what users need and design more intuitive digital interactions. Advanced Web Development: Develop richer, more capable websites and web applications using modern frameworks. Advanced Databases: Learn how to represent more complex relationships between data and how to access that information. Web Design Frameworks: Use design and development frameworks to create consistent, stylish web interfaces.

### 09. Year 2 Semester 2
YEAR 2 SEMESTER 2. Creative Coding: Develop more advanced coding skills to create expressive visual and interactive work. Software Project: Combine all your skills to take a software idea through planning, development and testing. Architecture + Operating Systems: Understand the hardware and operating systems behind digital applications. IADT Year 2 Elective: Explore a different discipline area and broaden your perspective.

### 10. Year 3 Semester 1
YEAR 3 SEMESTER 1. Core module: Networks + Cloud Computing. Students choose TWO of the FIVE listed electives. Data Visualisation: Turn complex information into clear, engaging visual stories. AI Engineering 1: Build AI-powered applications with large language models, prompting and retrieval techniques. Games Development: Combine programming and game design to build engaging games. Full Stack Development: Build complete web apps, connecting interfaces, server-side logic and databases. Mobile Development: Design and develop native digital experiences for mobile devices.

### 11. Year 3 Semester 2
YEAR 3 SEMESTER 2. Professional Practice + Placement: Apply your skills in a workplace setting and experience professional practice. Business + Entrepreneurship for Creative Practice: Learn how creative ideas can develop into viable products or ventures. Emerging Technologies + Trends: Investigate new technologies and how they could shape future digital experiences. Discuss the workplace placement or project/Erasmus possibilities accurately; individual routes should be confirmed by the programme team.

### 12. Year 4 Semester 1
YEAR 4 SEMESTER 1. Develop your specialism. Core module: Research + Analytics. Students choose TWO of the FIVE listed electives. Web Development + Delivery: Take web apps from development to launch, using deployment, testing, security and performance optimisation. AI Engineering 2: Build intelligent agents, customise models and engineer sophisticated AI systems. Applied Data Science: Analyse data to discover patterns, draw insights and support decisions. Virtual Reality + Augmented Reality: Explore immersive experiences or digital content layered onto the real world. Physical Interaction: Build devices using Arduino, Raspberry Pi, sensors, motors, LEDs and electronics.

### 13. Year 4 Semester 2
YEAR 4 SEMESTER 2. Major Project: Create a substantial independent project that brings together your strengths. Professional Development + Critical Thinking: Prepare for work or further study through critical reflection and professional skills development. Explain the major project can showcase the area the student wants to pursue and becomes part of their professional portfolio.
